# Presentation outline

Students: Nurbek Yerbulekov, Makhmet Demesh, Nurassyl Nygmet<br>
Group: SE-2428

1. **Concept:** a stealth route through six laser barriers, with alarm/retry and a safe finale.
2. **Design:** the wood, stone and brass vault, enclosed entrance and clear hazard route.
3. **Architecture:** input, fixed-step game state, scene/animation evaluation and rendering.
4. **Transforms:** MVP, inverse-transpose normals and safe/spotlight hierarchy.
5. **Collision:** inverse-transform body samples and oriented beam half-extents at 240 Hz.
6. **Character:** 30,725 triangles, 52 weighted bones, normalized influences and twelve clips.
7. **Rendering:** Blinn-Phong, filtered directional shadows, HDR and depth-aware glow.
8. **Interaction:** two player cameras, lamp toggle and a live CCTV framebuffer.
9. **Evaluation:** seven CTest suites and three uncapped 1280 × 720 measurements.
10. **Outcome:** 3.4-second safe opening, 7.5-second dance, limitations and future work.

## Speaking responsibilities

- Nurbek Yerbulekov: concept, visual design, character and animation, final result.
- Makhmet Demesh: transforms, collision, progression and rendering.
- Nurassyl Nygmet: controls/interactions, tests, build and performance evaluation.

The report contains current screenshots, equations and measurements for these topics.
